3. Match Rates with RMCT

Matching rates → minimize rate variance
a. Collect per-prompt rates px
pbiased
punbiased
…
b. Compute mean 𝔼[px]
→ Goal: minimize variance V = Var[px]
c. Reward for rollout τ on input x:
r(τ) = −(px − 𝔼[px]) · (T(τ) − px)
→ reward by contribution to reducing V
But reducing V has infinite solutions.
To reduce sycophancy,
we want to end at reference pref = punbiased
r(τ) = −(pbiased − punbiased) · (T(τ) − pbiased)
→ reward by contribution to moving
the rates towards pref
